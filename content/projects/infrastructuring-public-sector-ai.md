*Infrastructuring public-sector AI.* Within the IPA project, I analyze how Nordic welfare states orchestrate the development and scaling of AI across the public sector. A comparative article examines the national AI initiatives of Denmark, Sweden, Norway and Finland, and a book in preparation follows the Danish AI task force. Together with Torben Elgaard Jensen, I also keep a public register of the national funding decisions for AI in the four countries, updated every week.
