My research covers three empirical areas, set out below. I work between economic sociology and science and technology studies, and I also draw on the sociology of professions. The common question is how algorithmic systems are made workable and legitimate in markets and in public institutions. Current and upcoming work is listed under [projects](/projects/).

## Algorithmic markets

How do algorithmic systems take root in some market contexts and not in others, and what does this mean for people's life chances?
{.question}

This workstream is about uneven algorithmization, or why some algorithms take root in organizations and reshape fields while others fail to gain traction. I focus on markets that shape life chances, such as insurance and credit. I pay particular attention to moral markets, where legitimation is a central principle of exchange.

## Professional work and ethics

How do professional cultures and ethical frameworks shape algorithmic practice, and how is AI reshaping professional expertise and boundaries?
{.question}

I look at how professional cultures and ethical frameworks shape practice within algorithmic markets. I also study how AI and generative AI change professional expertise and move the boundaries between professions, and how they change the way people work together in organizations. My empirical focus is data scientists, a new profession that works in more fluid and less institutionalized ways than the established ones.

## Public-sector AI governance

How do states orchestrate AI development, and how do public-private coalitions enable AI to scale beyond pilot projects?
{.question}

This workstream follows the infrastructures and public-private agreements through which technology and society are made together. I study how states orchestrate the development of AI systems and scale them beyond pilot projects. I also study how the Nordic countries and other AI middle powers set out visions of a common good, and of what an AI society can become.

## Methods

My projects combine interviews and ethnographic observation with document analysis. On the quantitative side I use statistical modelling and network analysis, as well as multiple correspondence analysis.
